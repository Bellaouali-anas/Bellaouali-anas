# 👋 Hi there!

I'm passionate about uncovering insights from data and modeling complex systems using advanced mathematical tools. My work focuses on areas like:

- 📊 **Data Science & Machine Learning**
- 🔢 **Mathematical Modeling & Optimization**
- 🔍 **Kalman Filtering & Bayesian Inference**
- 📐 **Applied Statistics & Numerical Methods**

I hold master degree in **Applied Statistical and Numerical Methods**, and I enjoy bridging the gap between theory and practice—especially in solving real-world problems through automation and smart algorithms.

---

🔧 On GitHub, you'll find some of my experiments, research projects, and tools focused on **optimization**, **simulation**, and **educational content** in math and data science.

💡 Let's connect and collaborate on building impactful, math-driven solutions!
